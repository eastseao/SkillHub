# Business Insights

## Insight Generation

### The Insight Formula
Unexpected finding + Business impact + Actionable recommendation = Insight

### Types of Business Insights

#### Performance Insights
- What is driving results
- Why targets were/missed
- Trend explanations

#### Opportunity Insights
- Underserved segments
- Untapped channels
- Emerging demand patterns

#### Risk Insights
- Churn indicators
- Concentration risks
- Competitive threats

#### Operational Insights
- Process bottlenecks
- Efficiency opportunities
- Quality issues

## Insight Prioritization
- Magnitude of impact
- Confidence in finding
- Speed of implementation
- Cost to implement
- Strategic alignment
