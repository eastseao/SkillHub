# KPI Analysis

## KPI Hierarchy

### Strategic KPIs (C-Suite)
- Revenue growth
- Profit margin
- Market share
- Customer lifetime value
- Return on invested capital

### Tactical KPIs (Department)
- Marketing: CAC, Conversion rates
- Sales: Quota attainment, Pipeline coverage
- Product: Active users, Feature adoption
- Customer Success: NPS, Churn rate

### Operational KPIs (Team)
- Response time
- Error rates
- Throughput
- Quality metrics

## SaaS Metrics Framework

### Growth Metrics
- MRR/ARR growth rate
- New MRR
- Expansion MRR
- Churned MRR
- Net New MRR

### Efficiency Metrics
- CAC payback period
- LTV:CAC ratio
- Magic Number
- Burn multiple

### Health Metrics
- Net Revenue Retention
- Gross margin
- Rule of 40
- Product-qualified leads (PQL)

## KPI Selection Criteria
- Aligned with business objectives
- Actionable (can influence)
- Timely (available when needed)
- Understandable (clear definition)
- Balanced (leading and lagging)
