# Authority Building

## Authority Signals

### E-E-A-T Framework
- **Experience**: First-hand knowledge
- **Expertise**: Skills and knowledge
- **Authoritativeness**: Recognized authority
- **Trustworthiness**: Reliability and credibility

## Authority Building Tactics

### Content Authority
- Publish original research
- Create comprehensive guides
- Develop proprietary frameworks
- Regular expert contributions

### Backlink Authority
- Earn links from authoritative sites
- Digital PR campaigns
- Broken link building
- Resource page outreach
- Guest posting on niche sites

### Brand Authority
- Public relations
- Conference speaking
- Award recognition
- Media coverage
- Industry partnerships

### Social Authority
- Consistent social presence
- Engaged community
- Thought leadership content
- Influencer relationships

## Authority Metrics
- Domain Authority / Domain Rating
- Citation volume
- Industry recognition
- Media mentions
- Backlink profile quality
