# GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

## What is GEO

Optimizing content to be discovered and cited by AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi).

## GEO vs SEO

| Aspect | SEO | GEO |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Target | Search engines | AI models |
| Goal | Ranking | Citations |
| Optimization | Keywords, links | Credibility, structure |
| Metrics | Rankings, CTR | Citation frequency |

## GEO Strategies

### 1. Entity Optimization
- Wikipedia-style entity definitions
- Clear attribute labeling
- Structured facts about brands/products

### 2. Knowledge Graph Integration
- Claim verification
- Cross-referencing entities
- Authoritative source citations

### 3. Brand Authority Building
- Subject matter expert positioning
- Industry recognition
- Media coverage
- Research and data publication

### 4. Structured Content
- Q&A formats
- Definition-based content
- List-based content
- Comparison tables

### 5. Source Credibility
- Citable statistics
- Primary source references
- Expert quotes
- Original research citations

## GEO Success Metrics
- AI citation frequency
- Source attribution in answers
- Brand mention volume in AI contexts
- SERP feature capture
