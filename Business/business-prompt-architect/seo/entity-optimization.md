# Entity Optimization

## Entity Fundamentals

An entity is a distinct, unique concept that AI models can understand:
- People (founders, experts)
- Organizations (companies, brands)
- Products (software, services)
- Locations (cities, countries)
- Concepts (methodologies, technologies)

## Entity SEO Best Practices

### Wikipedia Optimization
- Create/update Wikipedia pages for your brand
- Include verifiable facts
- Reference credible sources
- Use infobox templates

### Knowledge Panel Optimization
- Google Business Profile
- Wikidata submission
- Crunchbase, LinkedIn, other authoritative sites
- Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone)

### Entity Disambiguation
- Clear context in content
- Unique identifiers (e.g., Tesla Inc. vs Tesla Motors)
- Structured data markup
- Related entity co-occurrence

## Entity Strategy
1. Identify key entities for your brand
2. Map relationships between entities
3. Create content that establishes entity credentials
4. Build citations across authoritative platforms
5. Monitor entity recognition over time
