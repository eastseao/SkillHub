# Customer Persona

## Persona Components

### Demographics
- Age, gender, location
- Income, education
- Occupation, seniority
- Family status

### Psychographics
- Values and beliefs
- Attitudes and opinions
- Lifestyle choices
- Interests and hobbies

### Behavioral Patterns
- Media consumption
- Purchase behavior
- Brand relationships
- Decision-making process

### Pain Points
- Problems they are trying to solve
- Frustrations with current solutions
- Risk of inaction
- Decision-making fears

### Goals
- What success looks like
- Short-term vs long-term
- Professional vs personal
- Stated vs unstated
