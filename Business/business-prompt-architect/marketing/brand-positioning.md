# Brand Positioning

## Positioning Statement Template

For [target audience] who [need/context], [brand name] is the [category] that [key benefit], because [reason to believe].

## Positioning Ladder

### Category Ladder
1. **Category**: The arena in which you compete
2. **Competitive Set**: Direct and indirect competitors
3. **Differentiation**: What makes you unique
4. **Brand**: Your specific offering

## Positioning Strategies

### Benefit Positioning
Focus on the specific benefit delivered

### Price/Value Positioning
Quality at accessible price point or premium luxury

### Use/Application Positioning
Best for specific use case or occasion

### Competitor Positioning
Explicitly relative to competitor

### User Positioning
For specific type of customer

## Brand Elements

### Brand Purpose
Why you exist beyond profit

### Brand Vision
Where you aspire to be

### Brand Mission
How you create value

### Brand Values
Core principles that guide behavior

### Brand Personality
Human characteristics attributed to brand
