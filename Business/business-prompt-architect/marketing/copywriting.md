# Copywriting

## AIDA Formula
- **Attention**: Hook with strong headline
- **Interest**: Maintain curiosity, establish relevance
- **Desire**: Build emotional connection, show benefits
- **Action**: Clear, specific call-to-action

## PAS Framework
1. **Problem**: Identify pain point
2. **Agitate**: Intensify the pain
3. **Solution**: Present your product/service

## Headline Formulas
- How to [achieve result]
- [Number] ways to [solve problem]
- [Result] without [pain point]
- The truth about [topic]
- [Year] [industry] report
- If you [action], then [result]

## Body Copy Principles
- Lead with benefits, not features
- Use "you" more than "we"
- Concrete specifics over vague claims
- Short sentences, short paragraphs
- One idea per paragraph
- Use subheadings to guide scanning

## CTA Formulas
- [Get/Achieve/Discover] [specific result] [now/today]
- Start [verb] your [desired outcome]
- Get your [specific deliverable] free
