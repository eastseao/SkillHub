# Campaign Planning

## Campaign Structure

### Pre-Launch
- Define objectives (SMART)
- Identify target audience
- Set budget allocation
- Develop creative brief
- Coordinate with stakeholders

### Launch
- Execute across channels
- Monitor real-time metrics
- A/B test key elements
- Respond to feedback

### Post-Launch
- Analyze performance data
- Document learnings
- Create case study
- Plan optimization

## Channel Strategy

| Channel | Best For | Typical CPL | Funnel Stage |
|---------|----------|-------------|--------------|
| Search Ads | High intent | Medium | Consideration |
| Social Ads | Awareness | Low-Medium | All stages |
| Display Ads | Retargeting | Low | Decision |
| Email | Nurturing | Very Low | All stages |
| Content | SEO/Authority | Low | Awareness |

## Campaign Metrics

### Awareness Metrics
- Reach, impressions
- Brand recall
- Share of voice
- Website traffic

### Consideration Metrics
- Engagement rate
- Time on site
- Pages per session
- Lead generation

### Conversion Metrics
- CTR, conversion rate
- Cost per acquisition
- ROAS, ROI
- Customer LTV
