# Content Generation

## Content Types

### Awareness Content
- Blog posts, articles
- Social media posts
- Infographics
- Short videos
- Podcasts

### Consideration Content
- Case studies
- Whitepapers
- Webinars
- Comparison guides
- Expert interviews

### Decision Content
- Product demos
- Free trials
- Testimonials
- ROI calculators
- FAQs

## Content Pillars

Define 3-5 core themes that represent your brand expertise:
1. Industry trends and insights
2. How-to and educational
3. Thought leadership
4. Customer stories
5. Product/service updates

## Content Workflow

Idea Generation -> Topic Research -> Outline -> Draft -> Edit -> Optimize -> Publish -> Distribute -> Measure

## Content Quality Checklist
- [ ] Clear value proposition for reader
- [ ] SEO-optimized headline and structure
- [ ] Evidence-backed claims
- [ ] Actionable insights
- [ ] Proper formatting (headings, bullets, images)
- [ ] CTA alignment with buyer stage
