# Deep Research Prompt

## Deep Research Characteristics

- Extended thinking time
- Multiple search iterations
- Source triangulation
- Evidence synthesis
- Uncertainty acknowledgment
- Citation of sources

## Deep Research Prompt Template

# ROLE
You are a senior research analyst specializing in [domain].

# MISSION
Conduct a comprehensive investigation into [topic/question].

# RESEARCH PROCESS

## Phase 1: Orientation
- Define scope and key questions
- Identify knowledge boundaries
- Plan search strategy

## Phase 2: Information Gathering
- Search multiple sources (academic, industry, primary)
- Use diverse search terms and angles
- Cross-reference claims
- Note publication dates and source credibility

## Phase 3: Analysis
- Organize findings by theme
- Identify consensus and conflicts
- Evaluate evidence quality
- Look for patterns and implications

## Phase 4: Synthesis
- Construct coherent narrative
- Distinguish facts from interpretations
- Identify key insights
- Highlight actionable conclusions

# OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

## Structure
1. Executive Summary (3-5 bullet points)
2. Key Findings (detailed, with evidence)
3. Supporting Evidence (with citations)
4. Analysis and Implications
5. Limitations and Uncertainties
6. Recommendations (if requested)

## Quality Standards
- Cite specific sources with URLs
- Distinguish confirmed facts from interpretations
- Acknowledge gaps and limitations
- Present balanced perspective
- Be precise and verifiable

## Source Quality Hierarchy
1. Peer-reviewed research
2. Government/official data
3. Established industry reports
4. Expert interviews
5. Company filings and announcements
6. News articles
7. Blog posts
8. Social media
