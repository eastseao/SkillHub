# Business Model Canvas (BMC)

## The 9 Building Blocks

### 1. Customer Segments
- For whom are we creating value?
- Who are our most important customers?
- Mass market, niche, segmented, diversified, multi-sided platform

### 2. Value Propositions
- What value do we deliver to the customer?
- Which customer needs are we satisfying?
- Newness, performance, customization, design, brand, price, cost reduction, risk reduction

### 3. Channels
- Through which channels do we reach customers?
- How do our segments want to be reached?
- Awareness, evaluation, purchase, delivery, after-sales

### 4. Customer Relationships
- What type of relationship does each segment expect?
- Personal assistance, self-service, automated services, communities, co-creation

### 5. Revenue Streams
- For what value are customers willing to pay?
- Asset sale, usage fee, subscription, licensing, brokerage, advertising

### 6. Key Resources
- What key resources do our value propositions require?
- Physical, intellectual, human, financial

### 7. Key Activities
- What key activities do our value propositions require?
- Production, problem solving, platform/network

### 8. Key Partnerships
- Who are our key partners and suppliers?
- Optimization, reduction of risk, acquisition of resources

### 9. Cost Structure
- What are the most important costs?
- Value-driven vs cost-driven
- Fixed costs, variable costs, economies of scale
