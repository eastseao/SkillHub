# Porter Five Forces Analysis

## The Five Forces

### 1. Threat of New Entrants
- Barriers to entry (capital, regulation, technology)
- Economies of scale
- Brand loyalty
- Access to distribution channels
- Expected retaliation

### 2. Bargaining Power of Suppliers
- Supplier concentration
- Uniqueness of inputs
- Switching costs
- Substitutability
- Buyer-supplier interdependence

### 3. Bargaining Power of Buyers
- Buyer concentration
- Volume importance
- Price sensitivity
- Switching costs
- Ability to substitute
- Price comparison capability

### 4. Threat of Substitutes
- Number of substitutes
- Quality of substitutes
- Relative price performance
- Perceived differentiation
- Switching costs for users

### 5. Competitive Rivalry
- Number of competitors
- Industry concentration
- Industry growth
- Industry structure
- Exit barriers
- Diversity of competitors

## Force Strength Assessment

| Force | Weak | Moderate | Strong |
|-------|------|----------|--------|
| New Entrants | High barriers | Some barriers | Low barriers |
| Supplier Power | Fragmented | Moderate | Concentrated |
| Buyer Power | Fragmented | Moderate | Concentrated |
| Substitutes | Few/poor | Some/average | Many/good |
| Rivalry | Low | Medium | Intense |
