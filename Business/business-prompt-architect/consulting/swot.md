# SWOT Analysis

## Framework Overview

SWOT examines both internal and external factors affecting an organization.

## Internal Factors

### Strengths (Positive Internal)
- Unique capabilities or resources
- Competitive advantages
- Strong brand equity
- Skilled workforce
- Technology advantages
- Financial resources
- Market position

### Weaknesses (Negative Internal)
- Resource gaps
- Process inefficiencies
- Skill deficits
- Brand vulnerabilities
- Financial constraints
- Operational limitations

## External Factors

### Opportunities (Positive External)
- Market expansion
- New customer segments
- Technology advances
- Regulatory changes
- Partnership possibilities
- Market trends favoring you

### Threats (Negative External)
- Intensifying competition
- Market disruptions
- Regulatory challenges
- Economic downturns
- Technology substitution
- Changing consumer behavior

## SWOT Matrix

|  | Helpful | Harmful |
|--|---------|---------|
| **Internal** | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| **External** | Opportunities | Threats |

## SWOT-TIES Analysis

Convert SWOT to strategic actions:
- **TIES**: Timely, Integrated, Effective, Sustaining

### SO Strategies (Maxi-Maxi)
Use strengths to seize opportunities

### WO Strategies (Mini-Maxi)
Overcome weaknesses by leveraging opportunities

### ST Strategies (Maxi-Mini)
Use strengths to avoid threats

### WT Strategies (Mini-Mini)
Minimize weaknesses and avoid threats
