### Technology Trends

- Cloud migration and edge computing
- Quantum computing emergence
- Biotech and life sciences breakthroughs

### Policy and Regulatory Trends
- Data sovereignty laws
- Carbon neutrality commitments
- Antitrust scrutiny of tech giants
- Digital tax frameworks

### Consumer Trends
- Values-based purchasing
- Experience over ownership
- Health and wellness prioritization
- Convenience and time savings

### Capital Markets Trends
- ESG investing growth
- Private market expansion
- Cryptocurrencies and DeFi
- Impact investing

### AI-Specific Trends
- Foundation model capabilities
- Agentic AI systems
- Multimodal interfaces
- AI regulation and governance

## Trend Analysis Framework

### Signal Detection
- Early warning indicators
- Weak signals in fringe communities
- Anomalies in data
- Expert predictions

### Trend Classification
- **Mega-trends**: Long-term, macro shifts
- **Macro-trends**: 5-10 year industry shifts
- **Micro-trends**: Short-term, niche movements

### Impact Assessment
- Magnitude of change
- Velocity of adoption
- Reach across sectors
- Timing and duration

### Source Tracking
- Academic research
- Startup funding patterns
- Patent filings
- Conference themes
- Media coverage trajectory
