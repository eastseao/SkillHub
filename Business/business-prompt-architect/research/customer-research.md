# Customer Research

## Voice of Customer (VoC) Methods

### Direct Feedback
- Customer surveys
- Post-purchase surveys
- NPS and CSAT tracking
- Customer advisory boards

### Indirect Feedback
- Support ticket analysis
- Social media monitoring
- Review site analysis
- Forum and community discussions

### Passive Observation
- Usage analytics
- Heatmaps and session recordings
- A/B testing results
- Feature adoption data

## Customer Journey Mapping

### Stages
1. **Awareness**: Problem recognition
2. **Consideration**: Solution research
3. **Purchase**: Transaction
4. **Retention**: Onboarding and support
5. **Advocacy**: Referral and reviews

### Touchpoint Inventory
For each stage, document:
- Channel/moment
- Customer actions
- Customer thoughts
- Emotions (frustration/satisfaction)
- Opportunities

## Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)

### The Job Statement
When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome].

### Job Dimensions
- **Functional**: Practical task completion
- **Emotional**: How I want to feel
- **Social**: How I want to be perceived

### Hiring and Firing Criteria
- What makes customers "hire" your product
- What causes them to "fire" it
