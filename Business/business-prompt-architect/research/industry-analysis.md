# Industry Analysis

## Industry Lifecycle Stages

### Emerging
- High growth, undefined structure
- Many new entrants
- Technology-driven
- Unclear business models

### Growth
- Rapid market expansion
- Increasing standardization
- Customer acquisition focus
- IPO window opens

### Mature
- Slowing growth
- Consolidation begins
- Efficiency focus
- Price competition

### Decline
- Shrinking market
- Exit and consolidation
- Asset rationalization
- Niche focus
