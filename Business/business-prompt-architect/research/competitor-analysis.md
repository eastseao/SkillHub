# Competitor Analysis

## Competitor Identification

### Direct Competitors
Same product/solution serving same market

### Indirect Competitors
Different products solving same underlying need

### Substitute Providers
Alternative categories that fulfill same function

## Competitive Intelligence Framework

### Product Analysis
- Features and capabilities
- Pricing structure
- User experience
- Technology stack
- Innovation pipeline

### Market Positioning
- Target customer segments
- Value proposition
- Brand perception
- Messaging strategy

### Go-to-Market Analysis
- Sales channels
- Marketing tactics
- Geographic presence
- Partnerships/alliances

### Financial Health
- Revenue and growth
- Unit economics
- Funding status
- Profitability trajectory

### Capabilities Assessment
- Team and talent
- Technology assets
- Distribution reach
- Customer relationships

## Competitive Response Analysis
- Historical response patterns
- Dependencies and constraints
- Resource allocation priorities
- Strategic intent

## Output: Competitive Landscape Map
Position competitors on 2x2 matrices:
- Price vs Quality
- Innovation vs Execution
- Breadth vs Specialization
