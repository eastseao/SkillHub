# Market Research

## Market Sizing

### TAM (Total Addressable Market)
The total global demand for the product/service category
- Top-down: Industry reports, analyst data
- Bottom-up: Number of customers x price

### SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)
The portion of TAM your business can realistically target
- Geographic constraints
- Product fit constraints
- Channel constraints

### SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
The portion of SAM you can actually capture
- Historical conversion rates
- Competitive dynamics
- Sales capacity

## Research Methods

### Quantitative
- Surveys, questionnaires
- Market segmentation analysis
- A/B testing
- Purchase intent scales

### Qualitative
- In-depth interviews
- Focus groups
- Ethnographic research
- Customer observation

## Data Sources

### Primary
- Surveys and polls
- Customer interviews
- Focus groups
- Mystery shopping
- Test markets

### Secondary
- Government statistics
- Industry reports
- Academic research
- Competitive filings
- Academic journals

## Research Process
1. Define objectives
2. Identify target audience
3. Select methodology
4. Design instruments
5. Collect data
6. Analyze findings
7. Report insights
