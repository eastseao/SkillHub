# Business Analysis Frameworks

## Primary Frameworks

### SWOT Analysis
- **Strengths**: Internal capabilities, competitive advantages, unique resources
- **Weaknesses**: Internal limitations, resource gaps, process inefficiencies
- **Opportunities**: External market chances, emerging trends, unmet needs
- **Threats**: External challenges, competitive pressures, market disruptions

### PESTEL Analysis
- **Political**: Government policies, regulations, trade agreements, stability
- **Economic**: GDP, inflation, interest rates, consumer spending, cycles
- **Social**: Demographics, culture, lifestyle trends, education, values
- **Technology**: Innovations, automation, R&D, digital transformation
- **Environmental**: Climate, sustainability, ESG, resource constraints
- **Legal**: Compliance, labor laws, consumer protection, data privacy

### Porter Five Forces
1. Industry Competition (intensity, number of competitors)
2. Threat of Substitutes (alternative products/services)
3. Bargaining Power of Suppliers (concentration, uniqueness)
4. Bargaining Power of Buyers (volume, price sensitivity)
5. Threat of New Entrants (barriers to entry, regulation)

### BCG Matrix
- **Stars**: High growth, high market share - invest heavily
- **Cash Cows**: Low growth, high market share - harvest profits
- **Question Marks**: High growth, low market share - evaluate
- **Dogs**: Low growth, low market share - divest/harvest

### Ansoff Matrix
- **Market Penetration**: Sell more existing products to existing markets
- **Market Development**: Sell existing products to new markets
- **Product Development**: Sell new products to existing markets
- **Diversification**: Sell new products to new markets

## When to Use Which

| Situation | Framework |
|-----------|----------|
| Strategic planning | SWOT, PESTEL |
| Industry analysis | Porter Five Forces |
| Portfolio management | BCG Matrix |
| Growth strategy | Ansoff Matrix |
| Business model design | BMC |
| Competitive positioning | SWOT + Porter |
| Market entry | PESTEL + SWOT |
