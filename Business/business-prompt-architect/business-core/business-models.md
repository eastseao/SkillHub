# Business Models

## Common Business Model Types

### 1. B2B (Business-to-Business)
- Enterprise sales cycles
- Relationship-driven
- Multi-stakeholder decisions
- Contract-based revenue

### 2. B2C (Business-to-Consumer)
- Mass market reach
- Transactional speed
- Brand-driven
- Volume-based revenue

### 3. Marketplace
- Two-sided network effects
- Matchmakers between buyers/sellers
- Commission-based revenue
- Critical mass importance

### 4. SaaS (Software-as-a-Service)
- Recurring subscription revenue
- Cloud-delivered software
- Customer success model
- LTV/CAC metrics

### 5. Platform
- Network effects
- API-first architecture
- Ecosystem development
- Multi-sided monetization

### 6. Freemium
- Free tier as acquisition channel
- Conversion funnel optimization
- Viral growth mechanics
- Premium feature upsells

## Revenue Model Dimensions

| Model | Predictability | Scalability | Examples |
|-------|---------------|-------------|----------|
| Subscription | High | High | Netflix, Salesforce |
| Transaction | Medium | High | Amazon, Uber |
| Usage-based | Variable | High | AWS, Twilio |
| Advertising | Low | Very High | Google, Meta |
| Affiliate | Low | Medium | Amazon Associates |
| License | High | Low | Oracle, Adobe (legacy) |
