# Strategic Thinking

## Core Principles

### First Principles Thinking
Break down complex problems to fundamental truths and rebuild from there. Avoid analogical reasoning that perpetuates past assumptions.

### Second-Order Thinking
Consider the consequences of consequences. What happens after the immediate outcome? Anticipate ripple effects.

### Competitive Strategy
- **Cost Leadership**: Become the lowest-cost producer
- **Differentiation**: Unique value that commands premium pricing
- **Focus/Niche**: Concentrate on specific market segment
- **Integrated Cost/Differentiation**: Best cost provider

## Strategic Lenses

### Time Horizon
- Short-term (0-1 year): Operational excellence
- Medium-term (1-3 years): Market positioning
- Long-term (3-10 years): Industry structure
- Vision (10+ years): Category creation

### Organizational Levels
- Corporate Strategy: Portfolio decisions
- Business Unit Strategy: Competitive positioning
- Functional Strategy: HR, Marketing, Operations, Finance
- Team/Individual Tactics: Execution actions

## Decision Frameworks

### Three Horizons Model
- **H1**: Core business optimization (now)
- **H2**: Emerging opportunities (near)
- **H3**: Future possibilities (far)

### Emergence vs Control
- Some strategies should be emergent (adaptive)
- Some require top-down control (aligned)
- Know which is appropriate for your context
